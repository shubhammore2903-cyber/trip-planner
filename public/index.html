🚌 Transport to Manali

Compare common routes. Actual schedules, availability and fares change by date.

🚌

Bus

Volvo / overnight buses commonly run from Delhi and Chandigarh. The last stretch is by road.

Planning estimate: ₹800–₹2,500 / person one way

Good for travellers looking for a direct road journey.

🚆

Train + Road

Manali has no railway station in town. Take a train to a suitable railhead such as Chandigarh or Pathankot, then continue by bus or taxi.

Train + onward road: varies

Allow extra transfer time; check the full route before buying tickets.

✈️

Flight + Road

Check flights to Kullu-Manali Airport (Bhuntar) where operating, then arrange onward road transfer. Flights can be limited and weather-sensitive.

Flight + transfer: date dependent

Chandigarh is another option to compare, followed by a longer road trip.

Tip: Compare total door-to-door time and luggage/transfer costs—not just the ticket price. Confirm live fares and schedules with the operator before travelling.
